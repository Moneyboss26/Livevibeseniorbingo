What Happens in a 5-Game Session

Every visit includes five complete, paced Bingo games using the same two large-print cards per tablet—no confusing card swaps between games.

Game 1
Classic Line
Warm-up round: any horizontal, vertical, or diagonal line.
Game 2
Classic Line
Second standard line round to build momentum and group energy.
Game 3
Four Corners
Fast-paced round matching all four outer corner squares.
Game 4
Double Bingo
Any two completed lines on the same card for an extra challenge.
Game 5 • Finale
Grand Coverall
Full-card blackout finale celebrating all session winners.

Common Questions

Activity Director FAQ

Do we need to connect to our building's Wi-Fi?

No. We bring our own dedicated cellular hotspot. Your IT department does not need to approve devices or share network passwords.

What if a resident has arthritis or trouble tapping?

Every tablet runs in Auto-Dab and Auto-Bingo mode. Numbers mark themselves automatically and alert the host the moment a resident hits Bingo.

How many residents can play at once?

We provide 30 dedicated tablets per session—enough for 30 individual residents, or up to 60 residents if pairs share a tablet.

Who provides the prizes for the winners?

Your facility supplies all resident prizes, snacks, or small gift items. We provide the entertainment hosting, tablets, and software.

How early does the host arrive?

Our Live Host arrives 20 minutes before start time to sanitize and distribute tablets, and handles complete collection and cleanup afterward.

What if we need to reschedule a session?

Facilities can reschedule any session at no charge with at least 48 hours' notice—ideal if a building experiences a quarantine or calendar conflict.
