Terms & Conditions

1. Nature of Service

LiveVibe Senior Bingo provides hosted on-site recreational entertainment, touchscreen tablets, and interactive bingo calling systems to assisted living, memory care, and senior residential facilities.

2. Service Agreements & Billing

The standard monthly service agreement includes 4 scheduled live sessions (up to 30 resident participants per session). Invoices are billed monthly according to the agreed service schedule. Additional sessions may be scheduled upon request at the prevailing single-session rate.

3. Facility Obligations

  • Space & Seating: The community provides an accessible activity room or recreation hall with appropriate seating and tables.
  • Prizes: In compliance with non-gambling social activity standards, any prizes, gift cards, or novelty items awarded to residents are provided entirely by the contracting facility.
  • Staff Supervision: Community staff or life enrichment personnel remain responsible for overall resident supervision and care.

4. Equipment Care

LiveVibe Senior Bingo supplies, configures, and transports all digital tablets and mobile networking equipment for each scheduled session. The host maintains full custody of the hardware before and after each event.

Privacy Policy

1. Resident Data & Identity

LiveVibe Senior Bingo prioritizes senior privacy. During game sessions, tablets only request a participant first name (or preferred nickname) to display on their temporary scorecard. We do not collect, store, or transmit resident personal health information (PHI), dates of birth, financial details, or government identification.

2. Facility Contact Details

Contact information submitted through our website or service agreements (such as activity director names, phone numbers, and facility email addresses) is used solely for scheduling, coordination, and billing. We never sell, lease, or rent customer contact information to third parties.

3. Local In-Room Network Security

Game tablets communicate over a private, temporary mobile network dedicated exclusively to transmitting game state and called ball numbers. No resident browsing history, personal data, or biometric data is ever captured or stored.

Entertainment Disclaimer

Recreational Social Activity Only

LiveVibe Senior Bingo is an auxiliary life-enrichment program and social entertainment service designed exclusively for senior residential communities. LiveVibe Senior Bingo is not a licensed gambling establishment, commercial gaming hall, or casino operator.

No Wagering or Paid Stakes

No real money gambling, cash wagers, buy-ins, or player entry fees are collected from residents or players. All games are conducted purely for recreation, cognitive stimulation, and community engagement. Any non-cash novelty prizes or recognition awards are provided directly by the hosting senior facility.
